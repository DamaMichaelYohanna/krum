1. Introduction & Scope

Krum POS & Inventory Technologies ("Krum", "we", "us", or "our") provides an offline-first Point of Sale application, inventory management tools, and cloud synchronization platform designed for retail supermarkets, provision stores, and wholesale FMCG merchants.

This Privacy Policy explains how information relating to you (the store owner, cashier, or staff member) and your retail store operations is collected, stored, processed, and safeguarded when using the Krum Android mobile application, the Krum cloud API backend, and related web services.

2. Data We Collect

To deliver resilient retail point of sale functionality, Krum collects the following categories of data:

  • Account & Business Information: Store name, registered phone number, email address, physical address, business currency (e.g. NGN, USD), and owner/cashier login credentials.
  • Store Catalog & Inventory: Product names, barcodes, SKUs, categories, units of measurement, packaging conversion ratios (pieces per pack/carton), cost prices, and retail selling prices.
  • Sales & Checkout Transactions: Date and timestamp of sales, itemized products purchased, quantities, unit prices applied, discounts, payment tender (Cash, POS Card, Bank Transfer, Debt), and void/reversal audit logs.
  • Customer & Debt Records: Customer names, phone numbers, transaction histories, credit ledger balances, and payment repayments recorded by store cashiers.
  • Cashier Shift & Till Data: Opening float amounts, cash drops, petty payouts, counted physical cash at closing, and recorded discrepancy explanations.
  • Device & Hardware Telemetry: Device model, Android OS version, Bluetooth printer connection status, and local sync queue state to troubleshoot offline hardware integration.

3. Offline-First Storage Principles

Our Core Promise: Your store operations never stop. Sales, stock receipts, and customer repayments are recorded immediately into your device's local encrypted SQLite database.

Unlike web-only POS software that sends every keystroke across public networks, Krum functions independently of active internet connectivity. All primary business records reside directly on the device you control. Local data is protected by device-level sandboxing and operating system security controls.

4. Cloud Synchronization & Encryption

When your device establishes an internet connection, Krum’s delta sync engine securely transmits queued local mutations to our cloud infrastructure:

  • In-Transit Security: All API communication between the mobile device and Krum Cloud uses HTTPS with TLS 1.3 encryption. Unencrypted plain-text transmission is strictly disallowed.
  • Authentication: Sync transactions are authorized via JSON Web Tokens (JWT) signed with secure cryptographic keys.
  • Tenant Isolation: Every store’s data is strictly partitioned by business tenant IDs in our relational PostgreSQL database, ensuring zero cross-tenant data exposure.

5. How We Use Collected Data

We process your store data exclusively for operational retail functionality:

  • To maintain your store catalog, calculate moving weighted average costs (WACP), and trigger low-stock alerts.
  • To format itemized purchase orders for your preferred suppliers via WhatsApp or SMS.
  • To generate printable PDF receipts and customer account statements.
  • To audit cashier shifts and identify cash drawer discrepancies.
  • To deliver periodic Profit & Loss, sales breakdown, and inventory valuation analytics.

6. Zero Data Brokering Policy

We do not sell, rent, or trade your retail store data, supplier pricing, customer lists, or sales figures to any third-party advertisers or data brokers.

Your margins, customer debts, and sales numbers are strictly confidential to your business. We will never monetize your store data or expose your sales patterns to external competitors.

7. Merchant Ownership & CSV Export

You retain complete, unabridged ownership of all catalog items, sales receipts, customer records, and financial reports entered into Krum. You have the right to export your entire business transaction history in standard CSV spreadsheet format at any time directly through the app or the cloud dashboard.

8. Cashier & Till Session Data

For stores with multiple cashiers, Krum tracks audit actions (such as voided receipts, stock recounts, and cash drawer reconciliations) attributed to specific staff user accounts. This audit logging is designed to protect both the store owner and honest cashiers by maintaining a verifiable operational record.

9. Data Security & Storage Location

Cloud database records are hosted on ISO 27001 and SOC 2 certified cloud server facilities with automated daily snapshot backups, automated failover, and strict firewall protection. Access to production database clusters is restricted to authorized systems engineers under strict multi-factor authentication protocols.
