Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about setting up and running Krum.

Does Krum require 24/7 internet connection to sell? ▾
No. Krum is built offline-first. Cashiers can ring up sales, print receipts, adjust stock, and reconcile shifts completely offline. All records are stored locally in SQLite and automatically synchronized to the cloud when internet connection is restored.
What hardware peripherals can I use with Krum? ▾
Krum runs on standard Android phones, tablets, and POS terminals (such as Sunmi). It natively supports USB and Bluetooth thermal receipt printers (58mm and 80mm), handheld laser barcode scanners, and camera-based barcode scanning.
How does multi-unit pack conversion work? ▾
You configure your base unit (e.g. 1 Piece) and packaging tiers (e.g. 1 Pack = 12 Pieces, 1 Carton = 24 Pieces) with distinct cost and selling prices. When you sell a carton, Krum automatically deducts 24 units from base stock while recording the carton selling price.
How do WhatsApp supplier purchase orders work? ▾
In the Restock Hub, Krum flags all products at or below their reorder levels and computes suggested reorder quantities. With 1 tap, Krum formats a structured WhatsApp purchase order listing product names, quantities, unit costs, and total amount, opening directly in your supplier's WhatsApp chat.
Can multiple cashiers use Krum simultaneously? ▾
Yes. Under our Pro and Enterprise tiers, you can create multiple cashier and manager accounts. Each cashier opens and closes their own independent shift session with distinct cash drawer reconciliation.
