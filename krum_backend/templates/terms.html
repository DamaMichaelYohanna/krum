5. Offline Sync & Transaction Reconciliation

Krum is engineered with an offline-first architecture to allow uninterrupted sales during telecommunications failures. The Merchant understands that:

  • Local transactions are stored in client-side SQLite storage until network connectivity is established.
  • Merchants must ensure devices connect periodically to the internet to complete cloud delta synchronization and backup.
  • In multi-device setups, temporary inventory discrepancies between unconnected tills will be reconciled automatically upon synchronization based on timestamp ordering.
