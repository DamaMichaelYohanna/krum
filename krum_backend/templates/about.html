Our Philosophy & Architecture

Engineering Resilient Retail Technology

Krum was born out of real counter experiences in African retail: where internet cuts out during peak checkout rushes, stock gets broken down into loose pieces, and till leakages erode business margins.

Why We Built Krum

In high-velocity supermarkets and provision stores, traditional cloud-only POS systems regularly freeze the moment mobile networks or broadband connections fluctuate. Cashiers are forced back to pen and paper, barcode scanners stop working, and sales data is lost or double-counted.

Furthermore, inventory in FMCG is uniquely dynamic: store owners purchase biscuits by the carton (e.g. 24 packs), sell by the pack (e.g. 10 pieces), and sell individual pieces across the counter. Without native multi-unit conversion math, inventory tracking becomes a nightmare.

Krum replaces this chaos with a robust, offline-first operating system. Your cashiers continue ringing up sales sub-second with zero lag, receipts print via Bluetooth, stock deducts accurately, and everything syncs effortlessly with the cloud when connection resumes.

Behind The Scenes

Architectural Superiority

A look under the hood of Krum’s resilient data flow and engineering stack.

Layer 1

Offline-First Mobile POS

Built with Flutter and high-performance SQLite. Every barcode scan executes a fast indexed query. Camera scanners, laser USB/Bluetooth guns, and manual lookups resolve in under 200 milliseconds.

Layer 2

Atomic Unit Converter

All product inventory balances are tracked in normalized base units (e.g., pieces or grams). When a cashier sells 1 carton of 24 pieces, exactly 24 base units are deducted atomically, preventing inventory drift.

Layer 3

Weighted Cost (WACP) Engine

Supplier prices shift constantly. Krum recalculates Weighted Average Cost Price on every restock receipt: `((OldStock × OldCost) + (NewQty × NewCost)) ÷ (OldStock + NewQty)`. Profit and gross margin figures are always accurate.

Layer 4

1-Tap WhatsApp Requisition

Krum continuously compares stock against user-configured reorder levels. When stock runs low, it auto-generates formatted purchase orders that can be dispatched immediately to supplier phone contacts via WhatsApp.

Layer 5

Cash Till & Shift Auditing

Every cashier shift begins with a recorded opening float. As cash, POS cards, and bank transfers are processed, the expected till balance is computed in real time. At shift closure, counted cash is reconciled and any discrepancy is flagged.

Layer 6

Two-Way Conflict-Free Sync

Client devices queue all mutations with unique client transaction UUIDs. When internet is available, delta batches are sent to the cloud Django + PostgreSQL backend, updating global analytics without race conditions.
